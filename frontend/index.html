How FloodShield works

  1. Terrain — Copernicus GLO-30 DEM, reprojected to UTM 42N (30 m / 60 m grids).
  2. Reservoir — official 2021 elevation–capacity curve of Machhu-2 (CWC/NHP sedimentation survey): 92.0 Mm³ at FRL 57.30 m.
  3. Breach — time-progressive trapezoidal breach (DAMBRK / HEC-RAS method) with reservoir mass balance; defaults from Froehlich (2008).
  4. Near-field SPH — 2D WCSPH solver (own code) validated against the Martin & Moyce (1952) collapsing-column experiment; applied to a vertical section through the breach.
  5. Hydrodynamics — LISFLOOD-FP 8.1, 2D local-inertial shallow-water solver (Bates et al., 2010) routing the breach hydrograph over the DEM.
  6. Impact — max depth, velocity, arrival time and Defra FD2320 hazard rating intersected with 95k building footprints, OSM roads/rail/facilities and WorldPop 2020 population.
  7. Satellite check — Sentinel-1 SAR before/after change detection (Python via Planetary Computer, plus a Google Earth Engine script) for the Aug 2024 Machhu release.
  8. Outputs — GeoTIFF, GeoJSON, Shapefile and KML for each scenario.

Key assumptions & limits

  • Copernicus DEM is a surface model (includes buildings/trees) and does not resolve the riverbed; depths near the channel are approximate.
  • Spillway assumed to pass flood inflow; breach outflow added on top (conservative).
  • Uniform floodplain Manning's n; no structures/levees beyond those in the DEM.
  • Population exposure = WorldPop 2020 people in flooded cells (not casualties).
